BetweenUs – AI relationship advisor

Point it at an exported chat and it tells you what's actually going on between two people. It reads Telegram and WhatsApp exports, measures the things you can measure (who writes first, how long the other person takes to reply, how the tone shifts over time) and hands those numbers to a language model that turns them into readable analysis, with scores for interest balance, emotional stability and communication quality. A second mode works on live conversations, drafting five possible replies and explaining what each one is doing. Nothing is stored anywhere. The chat is processed in memory and gone the moment the session ends.

Dyatlov Pass – interactive survival narrative

A text game about the 1959 "Dyatlov Pass" case, built on the actual evidence rather than the mythology around it. Real slab-avalanche model and comparable cold-weather cases first, then i turned that into mechanics: nine characters who each trust you differently, gear you hand out and can lose for good, and a hypothermia track on every character that decides who is still alive by morning. When your character dies you carry on as whoever trusted them most. 1,600+ lines of Ink, 89 nodes and eight endings, running in the browser through inkjs.
